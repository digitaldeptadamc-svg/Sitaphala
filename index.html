Department of Dravyaguna
Sitaphala
Botanical Name :

  • Annona squamosa L

    • Family Name :

  • Annonaceae

    • Local Name :

  • Sitaphala

    • Vernacular Name :

  • Hindi – Sharifa

  • English – Custard Apple

    • Synonyms :

  • Gandhagatra

  • Madhuphala

    • Habitat :

  • Cultivated throughput India, especially in tropical & subtropical regions, grows well in dry, warm climates & well-drained soils


    • External Morphology :

  • Stem: Small deciduous tree or shrub with grayish-brown bark

  • Leaves: Simple, alternate, oblong-lanceolate, green,aromatic when crushed

  • Fruit Round to heart-shaped, green with segmented scaly surface:


    • Useful Parts :

  • Fruit

  • Leaves


    • Rasadi Panchaka :

  • Rasa - Madhura, Kashaya

  • Guna - Guru, Snigdha

  • Vipaka - Sheeta

  • Virya - Madhura


    • Pharmalogical Action :

  • Antioxidant

  • Antidiabetic

  • Anthelmintic


    • Therapeutic Administration:

  • Atisara (Diarrhoea): Bark decoction is used

  • Vrana (Wounds): Leaf paste is applied externally


    • Dose

  • Leaf Juice: 10–20 ml

  • Root/Bark Decoction: 50–100 ml

    • Formulation:

  • Ramphala Churna
